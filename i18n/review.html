How this works (read once)
  • Left = original French (read-only). Right = the English that is shown on the site: edit it if it is wrong or unnatural.
  • Click ✔ OK (or Ctrl+Enter in the box) when an entry is good. Click ⚠ Unsure + write a note if you can't decide — Paul will look at it.
  • Orange boxes are notes from the first-pass translator (Claude) pointing at the real doubts. Start with “⚠ Unsure only”.
  • Keep placeholders like {n} or {name} exactly as they are (they get replaced by numbers/names). Keep \n line breaks. Names of items, mobs and NPCs are never translated.
  • Discord terms must match the English Discord app (“Manage Server”, “Manage Channels”, “Forum”, “Thread”…). Game/wiki terms follow the glossary below.
  • Your work is saved automatically in this browser. To send it: ⬇ Download my changes and give the file to Paul. To continue on another computer: ⬆ Load a saved file.
  • To see a text in context, open the page in English (link above, or add ?lang=en to any page address).
