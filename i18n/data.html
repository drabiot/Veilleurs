How this works (read once)
  • This lists the texts of the data sheets (lore, descriptions, quest texts…) that have no English yet, newest sheets first. Names (items, mobs, NPCs, zones, events…) are listed too: most need no translation, so click ＝ No translation needed (or ＝ Keep all shown as is for a whole filtered list).
  • Write the English and click Save (Ctrl+Enter). If the text needs no translation (name, proper noun…), click ＝ No translation needed.
  • Keep placeholders like {n} and markup like [npc:…|…] exactly as they are.
  • Then click 📨 Send to the admins.
